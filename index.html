FAQ

Questions

Do I pay per driver or per user?

No. Only per truck: R200 per truck per month.

Can I try it for one truck or one month?

Yes. Subscribe for a single truck for a single month. That also works if you only want to check a recent trip.

Do I need to be good with spreadsheets?

No. Drivers use a simple form and you read the dashboard.

Does a fuel flag mean someone is stealing?

No. A fuel exception is a prompt to check slips and odometers. It points you to where to look, and you decide what it means.

Where is my data stored?

In your own Google Drive. You can export or delete it whenever you like.

How do I cancel?

Message us on WhatsApp before your next billing date. No contract, no penalty.
