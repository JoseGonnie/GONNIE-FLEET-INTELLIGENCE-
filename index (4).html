Questions

Do I pay per driver or per user?

No. Only per truck: R200 per truck per month.

Can I try it for one truck or one month?

Yes. Subscribe for a single truck for a single month. That also works if you only want to check a recent trip.

Do I need to be good with spreadsheets?

No. Drivers use a simple form and you read the dashboard.

Does a fuel flag mean someone is stealing?

No. A fuel exception is a prompt to check slips and odometers. It shows you where to look, and you decide what it means.

Where is my data stored?

In your own private account, never ours. You can export or delete it whenever you like.

How do I cancel?

Message us on WhatsApp before your next billing date. No contract, no penalty.

How quickly will I see results?

Your workbook is set up within a day. Fuel flags and the dashboard start working as soon as drivers log trips, so most fleets see their first exceptions in the first week.

What do I need from my side?

A list of your trucks, drivers and regular routes. We load them for you and show your drivers the phone form.
